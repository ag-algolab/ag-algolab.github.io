For a school, a training centre, a club

A platform for your school, not one more piece of software.

Families enrol online, pupils work in their own area, teachers see their classes, the administration steers the school year. An institute with 7 sites uses it every day.

Describe my school See the case in detail

What's inside

Four areas, one foundation.

The account's role decides what opens. Nobody sees anyone else's screen.

The public site and enrolment

The pages, the level test, the enrolment form, the payment or the proof of payment. A family signs up without calling you.

The student area

Their classes, their calendar, their exercises, their progress. 24 screens for them alone.

The teacher area

Their groups, their sessions, their reports. They fill in what they must, nothing else.

The administration

Members, groups, scheduling, payments, alerts. 22 screens to run the school year without a spreadsheet.

What runs with nobody involved

Eight automations work through the night.

  • The class reminder goes out to the group as the session approaches.
  • A pupil absent for three days triggers a follow-up — a switch in the administration turns it on.
  • Dictations are voiced two times a day, in batches.
  • An evening coach sends each pupil what they need to review.
  • Your data stays inside the European Union: the database and the accounts sit on European infrastructure.

The price

A price per active member, or a flat fee.

The price is set after a first conversation, on your situation: how many members, what you already have, what needs taking over. You know what you pay before I write a line.
