The fact everything rests on

Since January 2025, a wrong answer no longer costs anything.

Right answer +4, wrong answer 0, blank 0: that is the scoring published by FNEGE, which administers the test. A blank gives up 1.33 points out of 600 on average; thirty blanks are close to 40 points. All of Prépa 600's messaging starts from this fact, checked at the source and not with a competitor.

Source: tagemage.fr, “Understanding your score” page. The site also shows the registration fees and the number of sittings allowed, all re-read from the same source.

The product

A free mock test, a full report, 6 paid mock tests.

The free mock test

  • 90 questions, six 20-minute sub-tests, five options
  • The timer is refereed by the server, not the browser
  • A pause is possible between two sub-tests; never during one
  • No bank card, three-field sign-up

The report

  • Score out of 600, computed by domain the way FNEGE does
  • Six marks, the time map question by question, the blanks and what they cost
  • The 90 corrections, each naming the option it refers to
  • The score's position among the papers, published only from 30 papers of the same mock test

The next 6 mock tests

  • €49 incl. VAT for 4 months, paid once, no subscription
  • Scholarship price: €20
  • Stripe payment, VAT included in the displayed price
  • The right of withdrawal remains whole: nothing is asked before paying

The free tools

  • Admission simulator: at what score each family of schools becomes reachable
  • Score calculator on the 2025 scoring
  • 6 revision sheets, one per sub-test
  • Scoring, dates and FAQ pages, written to be read by conversational assistants too

Under the hood

A static site, fifteen functions, a database, a checkout.

  • Static HTML pages, no build step; 16 Vercel functions in Node, zero dependencies
  • Supabase with Row Level Security: the question bank is never readable by the browser; the right answer only leaves once the test is over
  • Stripe Checkout and signed webhook; the payment opens access server-side
  • Resend for the results, Telegram for every sign-up, every finished mock test, every payment
  • A daily cron of three follow-ups (invitation, resume, offer), once each, capped, with a STOP
  • A manager dashboard: headcount, funnel (account → mock started → finished → paid), sources, reviews
  • A 65-check test bench that replays a whole test and the checkout, webhook signature included
  • A platform audit before every release: prices, versions, secrets, links, page promises against the bank

The question bank

630 questions written, and an audit that rejects a guessable bank.

A multiple-choice test gets gamed through the shape of its answers before it gets passed on substance. So every mock test is audited statistically before entering the database.

The official format

Question types, mix and instructions documented from the public practice papers. Seven writing rules, including: options of equal length, ten distinct instructions per sub-test, plausible distractors, never a reference to an option by its letter.

The statistical audit

No sub-test can be guessed better than chance by ticking the longest or the shortest option; the right answer is balanced between A and E; no mock test shares a question mould with another. 42 sub-tests out of 42 pass.

Logic in SVG

Logic questions are drawn in SVG, generated and checked: each draw is rejected if a rival reading of the visible terms points to another option.

No official paper is reproduced. TAGE MAGE® is a trademark of FNEGE; Prépa 600 is an independent service, neither affiliated with nor endorsed by it.
