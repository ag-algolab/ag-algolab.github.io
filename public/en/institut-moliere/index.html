Institut Molière: the whole site and the whole platform, built solo.

Institut Molière teaches languages and tutoring: 7 sites across one city plus online classes, from primary school to the baccalauréat, and adults in the evening. I designed, built and run the whole of its digital tool: the public site, the learning platform, the teachers' area, the administration, the automation and the commercial mechanics.

The decisions

Six decisions that hold the system together.

Security lives in the database, not the screen.

Access rights, by module, level and class, are enforced by the database engine (Row Level Security). A screen protects nothing; a rule in the database cannot be bypassed.

The level opens the library.

A pupil sees the books at their level, one step below and one step above. The explicit entitlement is only an exception. Proven on a throwaway pupil and three books, not deduced from reading the code.

The commercial model is instrumented end to end.

Free trial capped at seven days, a single extension per five-month period, active accounts told apart from trials, safeguards against unbilled access.

A notification leaves from the server, never from the browser.

The alert for a registration is sent when the record is written to the database. A safety net placed in the browser goes silent as soon as the connection drops.

Cookie-free analytics.

7 events, closed list, two funnels (test started → finished, trial opened → created). No banner as long as no ad pixel is set; never any tracking inside the logged-in areas.

A class's internal name never leaks.

“Français A2 | Amal L” is an administrative identifier. The pupil reads “Français adultes A2”, and it is the code's typing that prevents them from seeing anything else: displaying the internal name does not compile.

Marketing, too

A product is not enough if it does not sell.

I also designed the offer and the acquisition mechanics, and the tools the sales team uses every day.

  • The offer by audience and the price list, written in one place in the code: a price cannot differ from one page to another
  • The referral programme, proposed and built: one code per family, tracked to the registration file
  • The public site, its SEO, its structured data and its share images
  • The pipe that brings prospects from Meta ads straight into the administration
  • A prospecting and follow-up tool: contact import, qualification, targeted mailings with an anti-duplicate register, WhatsApp follow-up queue
