Prices · two formats · quote after one conversation

What it costs, written before we talk.

Two formats, two ways to pay. A showcase website starts at €500; a platform with accounts, payments and automations is quoted after a conversation, because it depends on how you work.

Format 1

A showcase website

From €500 to €1,250

Everything included: art direction of your own, copy written with you, a form or WhatsApp, SEO, domain name, hosting and going live.

What pushes the price up: the number of pages, a catalogue to keep up to date, one more language.

Format 2

A complete platform

Per active member, or a flat fee

Public site, online enrolment, member areas, payment, automations, SEO, written hand-over. The foundation exists: it adapts to your trade.

What pushes the price up: how many roles to separate, your billing rules, whatever has to be taken over from an existing system.

What doesn't cost extra

What's included either way.

  • The domain name, the hosting and going live.
  • SEO: tags, sitemap, structured data, share image.
  • The legal notice, and a site designed for the phone first.
  • A hand-over document: where everything is, how to change a setting.
  • One person to talk to, from the first conversation to launch.

How we start

Three lines are enough to start.

You describe your activity in three lines. I come back with questions, then a price. You decide.
