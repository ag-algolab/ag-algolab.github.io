The decisions

Three decisions that hold the system together.

Security lives in the database.

Rights by module, level and class, enforced by the database engine. A screen protects nothing.

The level opens the library.

Their level, one step below, one step above. Proven on a throwaway pupil, not deduced from the code.

A notification leaves from the server.

Sent when the record is written. A safety net in the browser goes silent as soon as the connection drops.
