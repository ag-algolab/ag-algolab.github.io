The fact everything rests on

Since January 2025, a wrong answer no longer costs anything.

Right answer +4, wrong answer 0, blank 0: the scoring published by FNEGE. A blank gives up 1.33 points out of 600; thirty blanks, close to 40 points. All the messaging starts from this fact, checked at the source.

Source: tagemage.fr, “Understanding your score”. TAGE MAGE® is a trademark of FNEGE; Prépa 600 is an independent service, neither affiliated with nor endorsed by it.
